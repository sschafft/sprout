## What it is

SMASH is the Symposium on Model Accountability, Sustainability and Healthcare, an interdisciplinary gathering focused on operationalizing AI safely and responsibly.

Its goal is to identify challenges and propose technical, ethical, and regulatory solutions to AI safety, data privacy, model interoperability, and accountability, explored through the lens of healthcare and sustainability.

The first edition ran in 2025. SMASH 2026 takes place November 3–4 at Mila in Montréal.

## Why the odd mix

Sustainability, healthcare, and safety are an unusual combination, and that is the point. Spaces that cut across academic boundaries yield compelling insights.

AI in sustainability and AI in healthcare are on parallel regulatory and operational trajectories. Both operate within interconnected ecosystems of stakeholders, technologies, and datasets, and share the potential benefits and the inherent risks, so each can gain from an exchange of ideas, governance, and tooling.

## How it runs

Researchers submit a summary and a non-archival extended abstract of one to four pages through OpenReview. Submissions are single blind, and there is no rebuttal period.

There are five tracks: ML safety, privacy, model accountability and alignment; interdisciplinary work from law and the social sciences; healthcare applications; methods for ML sustainability; and the intersection of sustainability, healthcare, and safety.

Every accepted submission is invited to present a poster, exceptional abstracts are selected for oral presentations, and a Best Paper Award is given in each track. The two days combine keynotes, invited speakers, a panel, a poster session, and breakout sessions.

## Guiding principles

Partnership over competition. Common ground over differences. Practicality over hyperbole. Questions over answers. Nuance over platitudes.

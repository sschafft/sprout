## Real inputs

Accounts sync from my personal finance management app into a local database. Nothing leaves the machine.

## Real models

5,000-trial Monte Carlo simulations with correlated returns, stochastic inflation, real tax brackets, RMDs, Roth ladders, and the ACA subsidy cliff, plus historical stress tests.

## Plain answers

Each screen leads with a sentence, not a chart: how likely the plan is to hold, and the next best move. Claude can review a scenario through the local CLI.

## The need

Surveyors measuring micronutrient malnutrition often worked without reliable internet, and teams in different regions didn’t have the same resources or know the same practices.

## On and offline

Through workshops with CDC we restructured the manual into a digital toolkit. The whole site can be downloaded to a laptop or USB key, and an Electron app runs it on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iOS.

## Launch

It launched in October 2020 with GDPR compliance and an analytics dashboard built in. Since then it has been used by surveyors in India, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Kenya, Pakistan, China, Indonesia, and Canada.

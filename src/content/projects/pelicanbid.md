## Reads

Drop in the bid. Pelican Bid pulls out every question, requirement, form, and deadline, with page references.

## Calls it

It scores how well the bid fits you, flags the fine print that costs money, and suggests a price range.

## Drafts

Every answer is drafted from documents you already have, with its sources shown. The few it can’t answer are flagged for you.
